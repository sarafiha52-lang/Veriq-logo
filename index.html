About the Project

A vision built around digital trust.

VERIQ is an Ethereum-based token project with a broader vision focused on trust and digital verification. Its proposed ecosystem aims to explore ways of helping users assess businesses, cryptocurrency projects, and online platforms through accessible verification information.

The verification platform and related services are part of the project's vision and should not be considered live services unless their availability is independently confirmed.
